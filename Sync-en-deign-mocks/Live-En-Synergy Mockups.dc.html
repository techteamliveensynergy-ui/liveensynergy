OUR STORY

Sponsorship, re-imagined.

Live·En·Synergy was born from a simple frustration: sponsorship rarely reaches the audience. We rebuilt it as a three-sided marketplace so brands can fund what matters, artists fill the room, and audiences get real value for showing up.

Frequently asked

How much does Live·En·Synergy cost?−
£315 + VAT minimum, or 9% + VAT of your sponsorship budget — whichever is higher. On a £4,000 budget that's £360 + VAT, leaving £3,568 to spend on artists and audience rewards.
