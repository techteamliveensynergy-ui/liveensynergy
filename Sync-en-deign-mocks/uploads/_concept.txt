Platform Pricing


There is no minimum amount that you can allocate for sponsorship budget.


Live-En-Synergy charges minimum £315 + VAT (£378 including) or 9% + VAT (10.8% including) of the sponsorship budget, whichever is higher.





Example 1: If your sponsorship budget is £1000: Our services will cost you £315+VAT (£378 including). So total budget available to you for sponsorship will be £622.





Example 2: If you provision £4000 for the budget: Our services will cost you £360+VAT (£432 including). So total budget available to you for sponsorship will be £3568.
