[Heading 3] Details
Opening and Arrival: Sakshi Gulati and Ketan Khamgaonkar checked in regarding their schedules and recent travels to start the meeting.
Campaign Package Structure: Sakshi Gulati outlined a tiered pricing model for the brand portal, proposing a starter package at 2500 for 50 incentivized participants, 3750 for 75, and 5000 for 100 participants. They suggested a minimum custom package price of 6000 to ensure sufficient margins, noting that any package below 2500 would not be viable for the business.
Campaign Dashboard and Reporting: Sakshi Gulati described the dashboard's functionality, which will allow sponsors to track campaign spending, ticket incentivization, and survey completion rates. They suggested that post-event surveys should be exclusive to premium or growth packages, and that the portal should provide audience insights, measurement metrics, and downloadable performance reports.
Campaign Creation and Administrative Role: Sakshi Gulati clarified that while brands will submit campaign details—such as budget, objective, and location—via the portal, the administrative team will create the campaigns to ensure proper linking between brand and artist profiles. Sponsors will not have the ability to create these campaigns independently.
Participant Incentives and Verification: To encourage participation in pre- and post-event surveys, the platform will utilize merchandise discount codes and platform points as incentives. Participant data will be presented to brands in an anonymized format, reflecting verified attendance and completion status. The portal will also support administrative features such as waitlists and newsletter subscriptions.
Artist Payment and Financial Workflow: Regarding artist compensation, Sakshi Gulati and Ketan Khamgaonkar discussed a split-payment model. They proposed paying a portion of the fee upfront and releasing the remainder only after the artist submits a report verifying the number of tickets sold, thereby ensuring the artist remains motivated to drive engagement.
Flexibility and Event Modifications: Ketan Khamgaonkar and Sakshi Gulati agreed that the system needs to accommodate changes to events or artists up to two days before an event takes place. Similarly, any requests by brands to change their budget or campaign details will be handled through administrative intervention and direct conversation with the team.
Operational Timeline and Performance Marketing: Sakshi Gulati explained that configuring a campaign takes several days to a week after payment is received. Once live, the portal will allow sponsors to view data. Ketan Khamgaonkar suggested leveraging past campaign data—such as the number of completed surveys and attendance figures—as marketing material to demonstrate success to potential sponsors.
Administrative Control Over Packages: Ketan Khamgaonkar stressed that the system should allow administrators to fully configure and manage campaign packages. This would enable the team to add, remove, or modify package tiers and custom options dynamically as requirements evolve, rather than being restricted to a static set of packages.
Survey Regulation and Quality Control: Sakshi Gulati noted that the platform must adhere to industry standards regarding market research, which are distinct from direct marketing regulations. To ensure high-quality data, they discussed implementing a "quality engine" to detect low-quality responses, such as identifying participants who complete surveys too quickly based on timing checks, or those who provide contradictory answers in open-text fields.
Technical Implementation of Survey Forms: The pair discussed the technical approach to building surveys, considering whether to embed third-party services like Typeform or Jotform, or to build a proprietary solution. Ketan Khamgaonkar confirmed that the team has the capability to build their own forms, and they will investigate the technical feasibility and data compliance requirements further.
